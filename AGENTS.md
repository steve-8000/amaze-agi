# Agent guidance

## Make work status clear

- When communicating about a task with remaining follow-up work, explicitly say what you will continue doing next. Do not end with wording that makes a still-active task sound finished.
- When the requested task and its agreed follow-up are complete, clearly say they are complete.
- When blocked or waiting, state what is blocking progress or what decision, permission, result, or external condition is needed, and whether you can continue any independent work.
- Apply this guidance to useful task replies and progress updates. It does not require routine all-clear messages, duplicate acknowledgements, or unsolicited automation updates.
